<p class="home-intro">Practical security research on AI agents, application vulnerabilities, and the boundaries that keep systems safe.</p>

I investigate how vulnerabilities work, what an attacker can actually achieve, and which engineering controls reduce the impact. The focus is on evidence, clear threat models, and actionable lessons rather than hype.

## Approach

- **Evidence first:** Separate confirmed behavior from assumptions and open questions.
- **Impact over labels:** Explain what an issue enables, under which privileges, and with what limitations.
- **Defense in depth:** Evaluate application, identity, network, workload, and infrastructure controls independently.
